What is Feedple:

Feedple is a social platform where people share ideas while training a personal companion that learns from their work, interacts, and inspires others.

Feedple is a social platform where people create, share, and continuously grow a safe, personalized companion that learns from their real thoughts, stays true to their essence, and remains accessible for learning, collaboration, and inspiration beyond their lifetime.

Our mission 
is to build a safe, personalized companion that learns from us and helps us achieve our goals.

Our vision 
is to create a world where each of us has a safe, personalized companion that learns and evolves from our thoughts, helps us in our tasks, and can be assigned to work on our behalf

What is Cortex:
Cortex is a safe, personalized companion that learns from its owner and helps them to achieve more.

What it Does:
1. It learns everything you write: Every thought you publish on Feedple continuously trains your Cortex.

2. It keeps working always (only if you allow): Your Cortex helps you with your tasks always, shares new insights, stays true to your purpose, and continuously spreads and teaches your ideas to others. People can learn from it, collaborate, and use it to solve problems.

3. You control it completely: You decide when to awaken it, what it is allowed to do, and whether it works automatically.
